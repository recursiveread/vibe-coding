Maple Hollow Lake

Wander through rolling grass and autumn trees, walk out on the dock, and give the blue birdfeeder a nudge.

Click or tab into the scene, then walk with WASD or arrow keys. Drag to look around.

What’s in the scene

It’s a sunny autumn afternoon in a wide meadow of rolling green grass under a clear blue sky with a few white clouds. Trees with orange, red and golden leaves stand among dark green pine trees, and fallen leaves dot the grass.

Straight ahead is a round blue lake. A wooden boat dock runs from the shore out over the water, and you can walk all the way to its end, where a white rowboat with wooden seats and oars is tied up. Click the boat to climb in and row around the lake; click the dock to row back and step out. A red bench sits on the shore to the right, facing the lake. To the left stands a stone sundial with a brass face; its shadow follows the real time of day.

Near where you start, a blue birdfeeder hangs by a rope from the branch of an orange tree. Walk into it or click it and it swings back and forth, then slowly comes to rest.
