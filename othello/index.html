How to play
  • Black moves first. Place a stone so it traps a straight line of your opponent's stones between it and another of your stones.
  • Every trapped line flips to your color, in all directions at once.
  • If you have no move that flips anything, you must pass.
  • The game ends when neither side can move. Most stones wins.
  • To share a game in progress, send the page link: it holds the moves so far, and your friend can make the next move.
  • Keyboard: ←↑→↓ to choose a square, Enter or Space to play.
