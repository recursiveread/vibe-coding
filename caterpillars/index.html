Glowing Caterpillars

Drop glowing caterpillars into the dark and watch them wander. When two meet, they either bounce apart or one crawls underneath the other.

  • Mouse or finger: click or tap anywhere to add a caterpillar.
  • Keyboard: Arrow keys move the target (Shift = faster), Enter or Space adds one.
  • Backspace removes the newest, C clears all, M changes colors, P pauses.

Your device asks for reduced motion, so the caterpillars will move slowly and gently, without wiggling or pulsing.
